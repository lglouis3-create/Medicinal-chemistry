# Change log

Newest first. The build embeds this file: the Topics page shows what is new
since the reader's last visit, and Settings shows all of it. One short line per
bullet, in the reader's terms — what they can now do or what is now right, not
which file changed. `style_check.js` fails on a bullet longer than 120
characters.

Every update adds its bullets here in the same commit that makes the change.

## 2026-11-01 (the 09/21 questions, and the shared drill engine)
- Slide references that pointed at the wrong lecture now name the right one (9 places).
- The site opens without asking for a name; wide tables scroll inside their own box on a phone.
- 92 more questions: 89 tell-apart items, 2 typed calculations with worked steps, and 1 matching table.
- Each question now names the skill it tests: recall, tell apart, apply, read a structure or scheme, or calculate.
- After a right answer, "Not sure, ask again soon" brings that concept back at the shortest interval.
- After a wrong answer, "I was sure of that" asks another question on that concept next, and again on a later day.
- Settings has three review paces: Exam cram, Weekly exams and Semester. Long term is now Semester, with the same days.
- An exam paper in progress comes back after a reload, and "Submit what I have answered" ends it early.
- Weak spots opens with this session's answers and a list of what to review next.
- The Guides are laid out one point per bullet, with side-by-side tables where two things are compared.
- Settings exports the questions (Markdown or JSON) and the Guides, Tell apart and Reference tabs (HTML).
- Each answer is also kept in an answer history, and Export progress includes it.
- A page left open offers Reload when a newer version is published; a reload returns to the same tab and question.
- On iPhone and iPad a card gives the steps to keep your progress in a Home Screen copy.
- Tell apart, CYP family and subfamily: the subfamily cut-off is given as slide 9 and slide 19 each print it.
